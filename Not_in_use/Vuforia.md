Vuforia is a powerful AR platform that enhances BIM by enabling real-time 3D visualisation, model tracking, and interactive overlays on physical structures. It allows construction and engineering teams to align digital BIM models with real-world environments, improving design validation, maintenance, and collaboration. Compatible with mobile devices and AR headsets, Vuforia streamlines BIM workflows, making project data more accessible and actionable on-site.

### Receiving and Accessing the Installation Email

We will send an invitation email to enrolled students at the appropriate time. Students must use the link provided in their email to proceed with the installation. Please note that this software is **invitation-based**, meaning you can only install it using your own email. If you have not received your personal invitation, you **cannot** complete the installation using a forwarded email from someone else.
