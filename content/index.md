### FAQ

> [!question]- Do I need a powerful laptop?
> The projects in this course are relatively small, so a laptop that meets the **minimum software requirements** is usually sufficient. We recommend referring to the **minimum system requirements** for **Autodesk** and **Unity** to ensure compatibility. 
>

> [!question]- Do I need programming knowledge?
>This course involves programming within a game development environment, namely Unity. However, the focus is not on learning how to code or write scripts from scratch. Instead, the necessary scripts will be prepared and provided to students. During the course, we will explain the rationale behind the scripts and teach students how to configure them to bring their interactions in Unity to life.
